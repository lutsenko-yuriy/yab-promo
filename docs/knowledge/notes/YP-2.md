# YP-2: Animated YAB presentation deck

## Notes

### 2026-10-22

**The vision sharpened once the whole deck could be clicked through**

The plan held through WU1 to WU4. In the final WU's review, with every section running, a
series of ideas reshaped the deck: Mordovorot back in as a case, a core-values section moved
to second place, "Not proven yet" renamed to "Plans for the future", a closing line instead
of "Thank you", a section menu with the contents slide, a fixed section label, and a hint to
turn phones sideways. Each was recorded as a scope change on the issue. On a greenfield
piece whose vision is still forming, this is expected rather than a planning miss.

## Debrief summary

### 2026-10-22

**What went well**
- The result: a deck the user is happy with ("a really nice page").

**What was hard or surprising**
- Much of the inspiration came at the end, in the final WU's review, and the resulting scope
  growth made the ticket take longer than expected.

**What to change**
- Nothing in the workflow. Late scope growth is put down to the ticket being greenfield work
  whose vision was not yet clear, not to the process.
