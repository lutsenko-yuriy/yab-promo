**What was hard or surprising**
- reveal.js turns its arrows back on with every key press, even when the slide change is then cancelled. So the right arrow is disabled again whenever the skip blocks a step. `Reveal.controls` isn't on the public API.
- The first menu screenshots showed no switch styles. That was headless Chrome caching `theme.css` from an earlier load: turn the cache off (`Network.setCacheDisabled`) in check scripts.
- Review: the class `.yab-menu-skip` read as the opposite of the switch's state. It was renamed to `.yab-section-toggle`.
