# YP-40: Share button: share the deck link with its settings

## Notes

## Debrief summary

### 2026-10-26

**What went well**
- Quick, with nothing to complain about for Yuriy. The path was plan (no analytics, as the ticket ruled out) → a verification checklist in place of scenarios → three phases, then two review rounds.
- The headless Chrome checklist was written before the code and failed first. Stubbing `navigator.share` and `navigator.clipboard` per case covered the share sheet, a cancel, a refusal, and a missing or refused clipboard, without a real share sheet.
- Each audit fix got a checklist item first. For the focus fix, the check was run once without the fix, to make sure it catches the bug.

**What was hard or surprising**
- reveal.js 6.0.2 writes `#/slide` to the address bar at most once a second, so a link read from the address bar can name the previous slide. The slide now comes from `Reveal.getSlidePath()`. `?lang=` and `?skip=` are safe, because reveal.js writes a relative `#…` URL.
- Desktop Chrome and Edge keep the page clickable behind the share sheet. A second pick fails with `InvalidStateError`, which now counts as not sent.
- In Chrome, a mouse click leaves focus on the button, and reveal.js ignores keys there. So Escape after a mouse open left the arrows dead, in the section menu too. Opening clicks now hand focus back to the deck.
- Review: the share panel duplicated the menu's open/close code, now one `popup()` helper. Both second passes asked for focus to go back to the button when the panel closes by itself. That was declined, so the arrow keys keep driving the slides.

**What to change**
- No workflow changes.
